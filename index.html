About the camp

WHY start with a placement test?

Most IELTS courses put everyone through the same program, whether they need it or not. But in our experience, what usually holds a student’s band score back is one or two skills, most often speaking and writing, while the rest is already good enough.

So we start by finding out. Your free placement test session shows your real level in each skill. Then we recommend a personal plan: a focused Speaking or Writing track (or both) to fix what’s holding you back, followed by IELTS Preparation. If your English is already strong, you go straight to IELTS Preparation, with no time or money spent on what you don’t need. Classes stay small (6–10 students), so every learner gets real feedback.

About the camp

What is IELTS Foundation Camp?

An online English program that gets you ready for the IELTS exam. We first strengthen the skills you need most, then train you on real IELTS tasks in our IELTS Preparation Course.

Who is it for?

High school and university graduates who need a good IELTS score for a scholarship, study abroad, or a better job. You already have an English background but don’t feel ready to start IELTS preparation yet.

I’m a complete beginner. Can I join?

Our courses are not designed for beginners. Book the free placement test and we’ll tell you honestly whether the camp is right for you now.

Are classes online or in person?

All classes are live on Zoom, so you can join from anywhere in the world. Your lessons, homework and tests are on our student portal.

Free placement test

Is the placement test really free?

Yes. Every student starts with a free placement test session, with no obligation to register afterwards.

What does it check?

Your speaking, writing, reading and listening, plus your vocabulary and grammar. We find your strong points and the skills holding your score back.

What happens after the test?

We recommend a plan made for you. Usually that’s a Speaking or Writing track (or both) before IELTS Preparation. If you’re already ready, you go straight to the IELTS Preparation Course.

How do I book it?

Message us on WhatsApp +20 155 702 5096, Instagram @ielts.campers, or Facebook “IELTS Camp”, or email ieltsfoundationcamp@gmail.com. You can also use the booking form on this page. The test takes about 10–15 minutes.

Registration and the student portal

How do I register?
  1. Book and take the free placement test.
  2. Fill in the registration form on our website.
  3. Pay for your course and send us the payment receipt.
  4. We email you your student portal login before the term starts.
What is the student portal?

Your online classroom. It has your lessons, vocabulary and grammar exercises, homework, tests, and your Zoom link for each session. You can also follow your progress there.

I registered but didn’t get my portal login. What should I do?

Check your spam folder first. If it’s not there, email ieltsfoundationcamp@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll send it again.

During the course

Is there homework?

Yes. Every session has homework on the portal. In the Speaking track you record your answers, and in the Writing track you submit your writing. Your teacher marks it and gives you feedback.

Are there tests?

The Speaking and Writing tracks each have a mid-course test and a final test. The IELTS Preparation Course includes full IELTS practice tests for Reading and Listening, auto-marked so you see your result right away.
