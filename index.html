About the camp

WHY start with a placement test?

Most IELTS courses put everyone through the same program, whether they need it or not. But in our experience, what usually holds a student’s band score back is one or two skills, most often speaking and writing, while the rest is already good enough.

So we start by finding out. Your free placement test session shows your real level in each skill. Then we recommend a personal plan: a focused Speaking or Writing track (or both) to fix what’s holding you back, followed by IELTS Preparation. If your English is already strong, you go straight to IELTS Preparation, with no time or money spent on what you don’t need. Classes stay small (6–10 students), so every learner gets real feedback.
